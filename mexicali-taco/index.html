About

Mexicali Taco & Co. is about good food: simple, well-made items that represent the northern Baja region of Mexicali, Mexico.

Our tortillas are soft and chewy, from recipes local to northern Mexican cuisine. Our marinades, salsas and condiments are made fresh in small batches.

Menu favorites

Mexicali Taco

Northern Baja-style harina or maiz tortilla stuffed with your choice of meat.

Vampiro

A crispy, golden "quesadilla" with your choice of meat and our special garlic sauce.

Cachetada

The classic tostada reinvented, with melted Mexican cheese and a savory chipotle aioli.

Shrimp & Fish Tacos

Adobado-grilled shrimp on a cheesy corn tortilla, or crispy-battered fish with cabbage, spicy crema and pico de gallo.
