About Karalee

Karalee earned her graduate degree in Counseling Psychology, with an emphasis in Depth Psychology, from Pacifica Graduate Institute, trained in imaginal, archetypal and Jungian psychology.

She is in private practice in Atwater Village and also runs small community dream-work groups.
