How Dorian works

My name is Dorian Clair, and I have been repairing antique clocks for over 62 years.

I polish all the wear surfaces and replace any worn material. When a part is missing or damaged beyond repair, I machine a replacement from scratch. With lathes, a milling machine and other tools, I can make almost anything, including gears.
