Our story

We are a family-owned operation that opened December 31, 1997. A brother and sister team found the shop and knew it was the perfect location for their dream. They hired a chef, and a month later the sister and the chef were in love. Four months later they exchanged vows right here in the courtyard.

The family tradition still lives on with the sister and the chef.
