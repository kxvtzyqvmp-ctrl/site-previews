About Leo

Leo had a distinguished career as an international ballet artist with companies including American Ballet Theatre, English National Ballet, Joffrey Ballet and Les Ballets de Monte Carlo.

Inspired by Kripalu, Ananda, Ashtanga and Hatha yoga, he created his own style, Zenciel: traditional yoga combined with the athleticism of ballet and the Chinese concept of chi.
