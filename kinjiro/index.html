About

Once upon a time, a boy named Ninomiya Kinjiro rose from humble origins and became one of the most beloved statesmen in Japan, bringing food and joy to the people during hard times through agricultural reform.

We, Nomiya ("Tavern") Kinjiro, strive to carry on our namesake's mission and bring happiness to our customers through good food and drink.

Find us in Honda Plaza, Little Tokyo. Cross streets: Alameda St and Central Ave.
